SIMPLE & QUICK

How to Place Your Order

Follow these four easy steps for a smooth experience.

🍔
STEP 01

Choose Your Items

Check the available menu, select your food and drinks, and confirm the prices before ordering.

💵
STEP 02

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your selection and pay the total amount. Keep your receipt if provided.

🎟️
STEP 03

Collect Your Token

Take your order token and remember its number. Keep it safe until your food is ready.

🥡
STEP 04

Pick Up Your Food

Watch for your token number or listen for it to be called. Collect your order at the pickup area.

A FEW HELPFUL TIPS

Before You Place an Order

Keep these simple guidelines in mind.

🕒

Plan Around Peak Hours

Lunchtime and class breaks can be busy. Order earlier when possible and allow extra time for preparation.

💳

Check Your Payment

Confirm your total before paying. Follow the cafeteria's accepted payment methods and keep your receipt if provided.

🎫

Keep Your Token Safe

Do not lose or discard your token before collecting your food. Ask staff for help if you misplace it.

🧍

Respect the Queue

Wait your turn, follow staff instructions, and leave enough space for other students.

ALMOST THERE!

Ready to Collect Your Order?

Follow these pickup rules to help everyone receive their meals smoothly.

  • ✓ Wait in the designated pickup area.
  • ✓ Keep your token ready and listen for your number.
  • ✓ Check your order before leaving the counter.
  • ✓ If your order is delayed, ask a staff member politely.
  • ✓ Clear the pickup area after collecting your food.
